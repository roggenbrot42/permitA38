# Specification: accounting (journal, charts, payments, period lock)

Status: **draft for maintainer sign-off** (release "VAT + accounting", v0.5.0-alpha.1; maintainer decisions of
2026-11-23). Author: architect (Opus). Review tier: **Full** for everything that posts, pays, credits, locks or
exports; **Standard** for schema, rules data and the guard. The implementer follows this document, does not decide
tax or accounting questions, and lists every choice it had to make under "Open design decisions" in its report.

Companion specs: [`ustva.md`](ustva.md) (VAT advance return, v2), [`zm.md`](zm.md) (Zusammenfassende Meldung),
[`datev-export.md`](datev-export.md), [`credit-notes-payments.md`](credit-notes-payments.md) (the API contract that
existing clients depend on). Oracle fixture: `tests/backend/fixtures/accounting_oracle.json` (written by hand from
the worked examples below, never generated by the code under test).

All tax and accounting statements are the architect's knowledge, not checked against the statute text of the year or
a tax adviser. Rules marked *(verify)* go to `docs/tax-adviser-review.md` section E and ship behind the banner "These
figures are not yet confirmed by a tax adviser" until confirmed. All figures in this document are fake demo figures.
**No account number appears in code or in this spec as a rule**; account numbers live only in the chart data files,
filled from the official DATEV chart descriptions the maintainer provides.

## 1. Goal

A freelancer or small business keeps **its books in permitA38**: every invoice, payment, credit note, expense and trip
produces balanced, unchangeable journal entries on SKR03 or SKR04 accounts. From the journal the app derives the VAT
advance return, the ZM, the EÜR (cash basis) or P&L and balance sheet (accrual), open items and a DATEV export for the
tax adviser. Corrections are counter-entries, never edits (GoBD).

## 2. Scope and non-goals

In scope: charts SKR03 and SKR04; income basis EÜR (cash) and accrual (*Bilanz*); VAT basis Ist and Soll in any
combination with the income basis; the seven tax treatments of the release (sales `standard`, `small_business`,
`eu_b2b_service`; expenses `standard`, `no_input_vat`, `reverse_charge_eu_service`, `reverse_charge_third_country`);
partial payments on invoices; credit notes, cancellations (storno), cash discounts (Skonto) and write-offs; trips;
VAT payments, special prepayment; Gewerbesteuer, income tax and corporate tax bookings; free manual entries; opening
balances and year closing (accrual); period lock; reports; DATEV export.

Non-goals: asset register and depreciation (AfA: a manual entry books it), bank import and matching, cost centres,
foreign currencies (EUR only), multiple businesses, payroll, the annual VAT and income-tax returns, ELSTER
transmission, partial payments on expenses (one `paid_on`), advance-payment invoices (*Anzahlungsrechnungen*: a
payment before issue is refused with a hint), the reserved tax treatments.

## 3. Vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| entry | one balanced booking (`journal_entries` row) with one or more lines; never changed or deleted |
| line | one debit or credit on one account (`journal_lines`); `amount_cents` positive = debit, negative = credit |
| role | a stable app name for an account (`bank`, `receivables`, `revenue_s19`, ...); the chart maps it to a number |
| tax key | stable app vocabulary for the VAT meaning of a line (`S19`, `RC_EU_SVC`, ...), section 6 |
| `vat_date` | the date that puts a line into a VAT period (UStVA, ZM); null = no VAT meaning |
| `income_date` | the date that puts a line into a profit period (EÜR / P&L); null = balance sheet only |
| booking date | the entry's date; decides the period lock and the journal order |
| open item | an invoice or expense with an unsettled amount; lines carry `open_item_id` |
| statistical line | a line with `amount_cents = 0` that only carries a tax key, a VAT base and a `vat_date` (section 8.2) |

## 4. Fiscal year and bases

`fiscal_years` holds one row per calendar year (fiscal year = calendar year in v1):

| Field | Values | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `year` | int | unique |
| `chart` | `skr03` / `skr04` | fixed for the year; a change only at a year start |
| `income_basis` | `eur` / `accrual` | GmbH (`company_type`) must use `accrual` (refused otherwise) |
| `vat_basis` | `soll` / `ist` | **default `soll`** (statutory default); `ist` needs the tax office's permission (§ 20 UStG), the UI asks once and shows the conditions *(verify)* |
| `vat_frequency` | `month` / `quarter` / `none` | `none` = exempt from advance returns; per year, so earlier years keep theirs |
| `dauerfrist` | bool | |
| `small_business` | bool | § 19 for the whole year; `vat_regular_from` (date, optional) ends it within the year |
| `rules_version` | text | the vat_rules / chart / datev file version used |
| `opened_at`, `opened_by`, `closed_at`, `closed_by` | | `closed` after the year closing (section 13) |

The settings keys `accounting`, `kleinunternehmer` stay for the year without a row (journal off) and as defaults for a
new year. **Automatic year opening:** when accounting is initialised and a posting needs year N+1 while year N has a
row and N+1 has none, `JournalService` creates the N+1 row in the same transaction, copying the bases, chart,
frequency, Dauerfrist and small-business status of year N (`opened_by` = the posting user) — the previous year is
usually still open in January, so its closing cannot be the only way to open the next year. A gap (no row for N) or a
year before the earliest row is refused (409). The user can change the copied fields under the rules below until the
first entry / return of the new year. A year's bases (chart, income basis, VAT basis, small business) change only before its first entry; later a change needs a new year. `vat_frequency`, `dauerfrist` and `vat_regular_from` change only while the year has no VAT return record (draft or final); a closed year changes nothing. Switching the income
basis between years creates a transition entry for open items on 1 January (EÜR → accrual: open receivables and
payables become balances; accrual → EÜR: they are carried as open items without balances) *(verify the transition
rules with the adviser; v1 refuses a switch while open items exist and says so)*.

## 5. Charts and roles

Files `permita38/core/charts/skr03-<year>.json` and `skr04-<year>.json` (package data and PyInstaller `datas`):

```json
{ "chart": "skr03", "year": 2026, "source": "DATEV Kontenrahmen SKR 03, edition ...", "status": "to_verify",
  "roles": { "bank": { "account": "....", "name": "Bank", "class": "balance", "eur_line": null, "automatic": null }, ... },
  "categories": { "software": "expense_software", "travel": "travel_costs", ... },
  "accounts": [ { "account": "....", "name": "...", "class": "pl|balance", "eur_line": "...", "automatic": "S19|null" } ] }
```

* `roles` maps every role used by the posting engine to one account. `accounts` is the list offered for manual
  entries (it contains at least every role's account; the maintainer may add more from the chart).
* `automatic` marks DATEV *automatic accounts* (the account implies a tax key); the DATEV export uses it, section 15.
* `eur_line` maps P&L accounts to the lines of the Anlage EÜR, with `deductible: true|false` (section 14, item 4).
* The `category → role` table replaces nothing in the expense model; an expense category without a mapping posts to
  `expense_other` and raises a sanity warning.
* A test checks: every role the engine knows exists in both charts of every year file; no account appears twice in
  `roles` except where the table says it is shared; every `automatic` value is a known tax key.

## 6. Tax keys

Stable app vocabulary (the enum leaves room for the reserved treatments). The mapping tax key → UStVA box and the
form rules live in `vat_rules/<year>.json` ([`ustva.md`](ustva.md)); the mapping tax key → DATEV BU key in
`datev/<year>.json`.

| Key | Meaning | Line kinds |
|---|---|---|
| `S19` / `S7` / `SX` | taxable domestic sale at 19 % / 7 % / another rate (`tax_rate` on the line) | `tax` (output VAT, carries the base) |
| `KU` | sale under § 19 (no VAT) | `base_only` (statistics, no box) |
| `EU_SVC` | service to a business in another EU state (§ 3a Abs. 2, § 18b) | `base_only` (Kz 21, ZM) |
| `VST` | input VAT from a German invoice | `input` |
| `RC_EU_SVC` | § 13b Abs. 1 service received from an EU supplier | `tax` (self-assessed, base) + `input` |
| `RC_3C_SVC` | § 13b Abs. 2 Nr. 1 service received from a non-EU supplier | `tax` + `input` |
| `NONE` | explicitly no VAT meaning (e.g. `no_input_vat`) | none |

Line fields for VAT: `tax_key`, `tax_kind` (`tax` / `input` / `base_only`), `tax_rate` (decimal string),
`tax_base_cents` (the base the line belongs to, stored **in the sign of the VAT effect**: positive for a sale or a
§ 13b purchase, negative for its correction, independent of the line's debit/credit side), `vat_date`. A UStVA box is a sum over lines selected by `tax_key`, `tax_kind` and
`vat_date` only. The box value of a `tax` line is `-amount_cents` (output VAT is a credit); of an `input` line
`amount_cents`.

## 7. Journal guarantees

1. **Balanced.** Σ `amount_cents` of an entry = 0, checked by `JournalService` before insert and by an integrity rule.
2. **Append-only.** `journal_entries`, `journal_lines`, `payments`, `period_locks` refuse UPDATE and DELETE at the
   database level (new guard kind `append_only`: BEFORE UPDATE / BEFORE DELETE triggers in every dialect raising an
   error). The transfer importer's replace-restore of a whole data folder is the only exception and drops/recreates
   the triggers inside its transaction.
3. **One writer, insert only.** Only `JournalService` inserts entries, with a plain INSERT through an insert-only
   repository method (a `Repository.save` of an existing id is an upsert and is refused by the append-only guard, so
   idempotent retries check `event_key` first and never re-save). Services call it with an *event*; the posting engine
   (`core/accounting/postings*.py`, pure functions) turns the event into lines.
4. **Idempotent.** Each entry has a unique `event_key` (`issue:<invoice id>`, `payment:<payment id>`,
   `expense:<expense id>:bill:r<bill_rev>`, `expense:<expense id>:pay:r<pay_rev>`,
   `credit:<doc id>`, `trip:<id>:r<revision>`, `vat:<id>` (VAT payments, refunds, special prepayment and its credit),
   `manual:<uuid>`, `open:<year>:<item>`, ...; a reversal entry has the key of the entry it reverses plus `:rev`). Re-delivering
   an event with an existing key is a no-op that returns the existing entry.
5. **Gapless numbers.** `entry_no` is gapless per fiscal year, allocated through `number_sequences` (doc type
   `journal`) in the same transaction as the entry.
6. **Hash chain.** `hash = sha256(prev_hash || canonical_json(entry without hash, lines ordered by line_no))`, with
   one chain **per fiscal year**: `prev_hash` of a year's first entry = 64 zeros (`fiscal_year` is part of the hashed
   JSON). A cross-year link cannot work, because the previous year stays open and keeps receiving entries after the
   new year has started. Instead the year closing (section 13) stores the year's last `hash` and `entry_no` in
   `fiscal_years.closing_hash` / `closing_entry_no` (set once; added by the E18 migration); verification checks each
   year's chain and, for a closed year, that the recomputed last hash and the entry count equal those columns (this
   also detects a truncated closed year after a restore). For an open year, verification also compares the last
   `entry_no` with the year's `journal` counter in `number_sequences` (a deleted last entry leaves the counter ahead).
   Hashed fields, in a JSON object with sorted keys: `fiscal_year`, `entry_no`, `document_date`, `booking_date`,
   `event`, `event_key`, `source_coll`, `source_id`, `source_number`, `reverses_id`, `counterparty_id`, `cp_vat_id`,
   `cp_country`, `text`, `origin`, `created_at`, `created_by`, `prev_hash`, and `lines`: a list ordered by `line_no`
   of objects with `line_no`, `role`, `account`, `amount_cents`, `tax_key`, `tax_kind`, `tax_rate` (decimal string),
   `tax_base_cents`, `vat_date`, `income_date`, `open_item_id`, `treatment`; absent values are `null`; row ids,
   `entry_id` and the `extra` column are not hashed. Changing this list changes every hash: only with a new chain
   version recorded on the fiscal year. Canonical JSON: sorted
   keys, no spaces, integers as integers, dates as `YYYY-MM-DD`, decimals as strings. `permita38 check-db` and the
   integrity service verify the chain; a break is an `error` finding ("journal changed outside the app or forked by a
   synced folder").
7. **Integer cents.** Lines store `amount_cents` and `tax_base_cents` as integers; services convert at the edge with
   `core/money.py` (`dec`, `q2`); responses carry money as **decimal strings** (section 7a).
8. **Dates and the lock.** Every entry has a `document_date` (the date of the payment, bill, invoice, credit note)
   and a `booking_date = max(document_date, first day of the first unlocked month)`: late documents are booked into
   the first open month, never refused for being late (section 11). **Closing date exception:** when that date
   would fall into a later fiscal year while the document's own fiscal year is not closed, the booking date is
   31 December of the document's year instead (a "closing date" entry, like DATEV's period 13), so late bills and
   year-end entries stay in their year after the December/Q4 return has locked December. The entry's
   `fiscal_year` is the year of its booking date. `vat_date` and `income_date` follow the rules of
   section 9 and may lie in a locked month only in the **allowed drift cases** of section 11 (the caller chooses the
  `vat_date` with the section 11 rules; `JournalService` itself marks every line whose `vat_date` lies in a locked
  month as drift — a caller cannot set or suppress the flag — and refuses such a line in a manual entry); such lines create drift
   on the return of that period ([`ustva.md`](ustva.md)) and a warning on the entry.
9. **Synced folders.** When accounting is on and the data folder looks synced (the existing sync detection, or a
   setting), the app shows "use permitA38 on one device at a time while accounting is on"; the hash chain detects a
   fork.

## 7a. Typing and money representation (maintainer decisions of 2026-11-23)

* **Strict Python.** `core/accounting/`, `core/vat/` and every new service run under `mypy --strict` (a per-module
  override in `pyproject.toml`, enforced in CI by `make lint`): no `Any`, no untyped dicts inside the engine.
  `NewType`s `Cents` (int), `Role`, `AccountNo`, `TaxKey`; frozen dataclasses for events, states, targets and lines;
  `Literal`/`Enum` for tax keys, tax kinds, bases, treatments and credit kinds. The posting engine is pure functions
  (event + state in, lines out). A test fails when an accounting module uses `float` or `money.to_float`.
* **Money in the API is a decimal string** with two decimals (`"1190.00"`, `"-57.10"`), in every response of this
  release and — by milestone M0b ([`decimal-money-api.md`](decimal-money-api.md)) — in every existing endpoint too.
  Inputs accept a decimal string or a JSON number (parsed exactly by `money.dec`); the new `*In` models of this
  release use a Decimal-yielding primitive `MoneyIn` (no float after validation). Percentages, quantities and hours
  stay numbers. The web UI formats the strings and computes with money only through `web/money.js` (cents as
  BigInt), never with JS numbers.

## 9. Posting templates

Notation: **D** debit (positive cents), **C** credit (negative). `N_r`, `V_r`, `G_r = N_r + V_r` are the net, VAT and
gross of rate group `r` from the **issued snapshot**; `G = Σ G_r`. `perf` is the performance date: `service_to`, else
`service_from`, else the invoice date. Revenue and VAT roles are per rate group (`_s19`, `_s7`, `_sx`).

### 9.1 Invoice issued (`finalize`)

The snapshot `issued` is written in the same transaction as the number: `{regime: "regular"|"small_business",
treatment, rates: {"19": {net, vat, gross}, ...} (cents), gross, perf, customer: {id, name, vat_id, country},
issued_at, rules_version}`. `doc_totals` uses the snapshot for every non-draft document (no retroactive repricing).
Rounding: each line amount is rounded to cents (half-up) first; the cents are summed per rate group to `N_r`; `V_r` =
half-up(`N_r` × rate). **Zero amounts:** a group with `N_r` = 0 posts no lines; a group with `N_r` ≠ 0 but `V_r` = 0
(e.g. 0.05 at 7 %) posts its revenue line and, instead of a 0 VAT line, a statistical line (amount 0, same `tax_key`,
`tax_kind` base_only, base `N_r`, same `vat_date`) so the base still reaches Kz 81/86 — under Soll only; under Ist the
issue carries no tax line at all and the payment posts the base (9.2); no `vat_not_due` or clearing line of 0 is
written, and no later event posts a 0 line other than a base_only statistical line. A statistical line carries the
same `vat_date` and `income_date` as the tax line it stands for (amount 0, so it changes no EÜR figure).
Small-business status of the year forces `treatment = small_business` and rate 0.

| Basis | Lines (one entry, booking date = invoice date) |
|---|---|
| accrual, Soll, `standard` | D `receivables` G (open item); per r: C `revenue_r` N_r (`income_date` = invoice date); C `vat_out_r` V_r (`tax_key` S19/S7/SX, `tax_kind` tax, base N_r, `vat_date` = perf) |
| accrual, Ist, `standard` | D `receivables` G; per r: C `revenue_r` N_r (`income_date` = invoice date); C `vat_not_due_r` V_r (no tax key) |
| EÜR, Soll, `standard` | per r: D `vat_soll_clearing_r` V_r; C `vat_out_r` V_r (tax line as above, `vat_date` = perf). No income line. |
| EÜR, Ist, `standard` | no entry (the payment posts); an entry with no lines is not written |
| accrual, `eu_b2b_service` | D `receivables` G; C `revenue_eu_svc` N (`income_date` = invoice date, `tax_key` EU_SVC, `tax_kind` base_only, base N, `vat_date` = perf) — Ist **and** Soll: Kz 21/ZM always follow perf |
| EÜR, `eu_b2b_service` | one statistical line: `revenue_eu_svc`, amount 0, `tax_key` EU_SVC, base_only, base N, `vat_date` = perf (an entry of only statistical lines is allowed; it balances trivially) |
| accrual, `small_business` | D `receivables` G; C `revenue_ku` G (`tax_key` KU, base_only, base G, `vat_date` = invoice date, `income_date` = invoice date) |
| any basis, rate 0 on a `standard` invoice | that group posts like a revenue group without VAT: accrual C `revenue_s0` N (`tax_key` NONE, `income_date`), no VAT, `vat_not_due` or clearing line; EÜR: revenue at payment (9.2). Lines with `tax_key` NONE are ignored by every VAT sum, the UStVA, the ZM and the DATEV tax keys |
| any basis, an invoice whose every amount is 0 | no entry |
| EÜR, `small_business` | no entry |

### 9.2 Later events on an invoice: one target rule for payments, refunds, credit notes and write-offs

Every later event on an issued invoice (a payment, a refund, the reversal of a payment, a credit note, a write-off, a
write-off reversal) is posted the same way: the engine computes **targets** for the invoice from its state *after*
the event and posts `targets(after) − targets(before)`, where *before* is the state just before the event (for the
first event after the accounting start on an invoice issued earlier: the pre-start baseline of section 16 step 4).
**All targets are evaluated at every event**; a delta caused by a non-cash event (credit note, write-off) takes that
document's date as `vat_date` and `income_date`. This is order-independent, idempotent and exact when the invoice is
settled. The issue entry of 9.1 is the same rule evaluated from the empty state (no cash, no credits).

**State** per rate group `r`: original `G_r`, `V_r`, `N_r` (snapshot); credited `K_r`, `KV_r` (Σ of the issued credit
notes incl. write-offs, minus write-off reversals); current `C_r = G_r − K_r`, `CV_r = V_r − KV_r`,
`CN_r = C_r − CV_r`; `C = Σ C_r`. Cash kept `P` = Σ payments − Σ refunds (reversal rows included); `P < 0` is refused.
`T = min(P, G)`; `P − T` is an overpayment. An invoice whose gross is zero or negative (a legacy credit invoice)
keeps `T = 0`: all its cash is customer credit, and under Soll its due VAT is the current VAT (`D_r = CV_r`,
`B_r = CN_r`, the negative VAT is reduced at issue); under Ist it stays 0. A snapshot whose rate groups have mixed signs (one positive, one
negative gross) is refused at issue (422), so the allocation always works on groups of one sign.

**Cash allocation** (cumulative):
* `T ≤ C`: `T_r` = allocation of `T` over `C_r` (largest remainder in cents, ties to the larger rate);
  `TV_r = q2(CV_r × T_r / C_r)` (0 when `C_r = 0`).
* `T > C` (cash kept above the current gross because a credit has not been refunded yet): `T_r = C_r` + allocation of
  `T − C` over `K_r`; `TV_r = CV_r + q2(KV_r × (T_r − C_r) / K_r)`.
* `TN_r = T_r − TV_r`. When `T = C` the allocation gives `T_r = C_r`, `TV_r = CV_r` exactly.

**Due VAT** `D_r` (the tax lines on `vat_out_r`) and its base `B_r`:
* **Soll:** `D_r = max(CV_r, TV_r)`: the current VAT, plus the VAT on cash kept above the current gross. A credit
  therefore reduces the VAT at once for the part not yet paid and, for the paid part, only when it is refunded (§ 17
  Abs. 1, UStAE 17.1 Abs. 2 *(verify)*). `B_r = CN_r` when `CV_r ≥ TV_r`, else `TN_r`. Evaluated at every event.
* **Ist:** `D_r = TV_r`, `B_r = TN_r`: the VAT contained in the cash kept. A credit note or write-off of an unpaid
  rest does not change it (an unpaid rest was never taxed; the `T > C` branch keeps the VAT of cash not refunded), but
  when a credit moves the allocation of the cash between rate groups (A18) the VAT follows at the credit date.

**Targets and the lines that carry their differences:**

| Target | accrual | EÜR |
|---|---|---|
| cash | `bank` (or `cash`) = `P` | same |
| revenue | `revenue_r` = −N_r; credits on `revenue_r` (storno, partial), `cash_discount_granted` (discount), `bad_debts` (write-off) = +their net; `income_date` = event date | `revenue_r` = −TN_r, `income_date` = the event date (cash date for cash events) |
| due VAT | `vat_out_r` = −D_r: tax line, `tax_key` S19/S7/SX, `tax_base` = ΔB_r | same |
| open item | `receivables` = C − T (negative = an unrefunded credit owed to the customer); `customer_credit` = −(P − T) | `customer_credit` = −(P − T), no `income_date` |
| balancing | `vat_not_due_r` = −(CV_r − D_r): a credit balance is VAT not yet due (Ist), a debit balance a reduction not yet effective (Soll) | `vat_soll_clearing_r` = D_r − TV_r: VAT declared but not yet received (Soll; always 0 under Ist) |

Dates on the lines: `vat_date` of a tax line = the performance date for the issue entry under Soll, the cash date for
cash events, the credit note date for credit notes and write-offs (§ 17: the period of the change, never the original
period). Under EÜR the change of `vat_soll_clearing_r` is posted as two lines: the cash part (−ΔTV_r, `income_date` =
cash date: the "VAT received" of the gross EÜR) and the declaration part (+ΔD_r, no `income_date`). Under EÜR/Ist the
`vat_out_r` line of a cash event carries `income_date` = cash date for the same reason.

Both columns balance by construction: accrual P + (C − T) − (P − T) − CN − D − (CV − D) = 0; EÜR P − TN − D + (D − TV)
− (P − T) = 0.

**Write-off reversal.** A payment that raises `P` above `C` while write-offs exist first creates a write-off reversal
record (a credit document with `credit_kind = write_off_reversal`, negative amounts, `corrects_id` = the invoice,
dated the payment date; event key `credit:<id>`) for `min(T_after − max(C, T_before), remaining written-off gross)`
— bounded by what **this payment** adds above the current gross, so cash owed back to the customer after a credit
note never reverses a write-off — allocated over the
remaining written-off gross per rate group (largest remainder in cents, ties to the larger rate), its VAT in
proportion, so a full recovery restores exactly the written-off net and VAT; then the payment posts. Reversing that payment later does
**not** restore the write-off: the receivable reopens and the user writes it off again if needed. Recovered cash is revenue again (accrual:
credit `bad_debts`) and under Soll the VAT is due again at the receipt date (§ 17 Abs. 2 Nr. 1 Satz 2); under Ist the
cash rule handles it.

**Treatments.**
* `eu_b2b_service`: no VAT (`V_r = 0`). The Kz 21 / ZM base is a `base_only` line with `tax_key` EU_SVC whose target is
  `CN` (accrual: on `revenue_eu_svc`; EÜR: a statistical line, amount 0), `vat_date` = performance date for the issue
  and the credit note date for credits. Under EÜR revenue follows cash (`revenue_eu_svc` = −T, no tax key).
* Write-offs and discounts under accrual always post their net to `bad_debts` / `cash_discount_granted`, for every
  treatment; for `eu_b2b_service` and `small_business` the Kz 21 / KU statistic is reduced by a separate statistical
  line (amount 0, base −net) on `revenue_eu_svc` / `revenue_ku`, dated the credit date. Under EÜR they post no
  revenue amount (no cash moved), but an `eu_b2b_service` write-off or discount still posts the statistical
  `EU_SVC` line (amount 0, base −net, credit date), because its Kz 21 / ZM base was reported at issue; the KU
  statistic under EÜR follows cash and needs no line.
* `small_business`: accrual `revenue_ku` = −CN with `tax_key` KU (`base_only`, `vat_date` = event date); EÜR
  `revenue_ku` = −T at cash events with `tax_key` KU, `base_only`, base ΔT.
* A line at rate 0 on a `standard` invoice (e.g. pass-through costs) posts on `revenue_s0` with `tax_key` NONE and
  keeps the existing sanity warning `vat_zero_line`; exempt sales are a non-goal.

### 9.3 Credit notes and write-offs (the documents; their postings follow 9.2)

Credit notes are issued documents of `kind = credit_note` with their own number (sequence `credit_note`), lines
with **positive** amounts meaning "credited", `corrects_id` = the invoice and their own snapshot; they may not exceed
the current gross per rate group (422). `storno` credits all remaining lines; `partial` the given net per rate group;
`discount` (Skonto) is created by "settle with discount" **before** the payment it settles, with the amounts
`C_r − T'_r` (`T'` = the allocation of the cash after that payment over the current `C_r`), so the payment then
settles exactly; `write_off` credits the open amount `C_r − T_r` per rate group (§ 17 Abs. 2). The VAT of a write-off
or discount credit is the complement `CV_r − TV_r` (not gross × r / (100 + r)), so no cent stays on `vat_not_due`. Write-offs and their
reversals are internal vouchers (no PDF, `Belegfeld` = the journal entry number).

### 9.4 Expenses

Per expense line (classic expense = one implicit line, itemised = `expense_lines()`): `n`, `v`, rate `r`, income share
`pct` (`deductible`), VAT share `vp` (`vat_deductible`, else `pct`). `iv = q2(v × vp / 100)` is the deductible input
VAT, `nd = v − iv` its non-deductible rest (§ 9b EStG: part of the cost). Cost `c = n + nd`; the deductible cost
`cd = q2(c × pct / 100)` goes to the category role; the rest `c − cd` goes to `meals_nondeductible` for meals (a
business expense that is not deductible) and to `private_withdrawal` for every other category (the private share).
Gross `g = n + v`.

An expense posts **two events**: the bill (`expense:<id>:bill:r<n>`, document date = expense `date`) and the payment
(`expense:<id>:pay:r<n>`, document date = `paid_on`). An empty `paid_on` means **unpaid** under accrual (an open
payable, no payment entry). Under EÜR it is assumed paid on `date`, as the app counts today, with a hint; the
assumption is a payment entry like any other. Setting or changing only `paid_on` reverses and reposts the payment
event alone (the payment revision increases, the bill revision stays). A reversal entry has the event key of the
entry it reverses plus `:rev`. Payment entries carry no VAT line, so reversing one whose booking date lies in a
locked month is allowed: the reversal and the new entry are booked in the first open month.
Whether a fiscal year is closed is passed in by the caller from `fiscal_years.closed_at` (section 13); a locked month
never closes a year. Input VAT `vat_date` = the expense `date` (proxy for the receipt of the invoice *(verify)*; year-versioned in the rules
file because of the change announced for 2028 *(verify)*); when that month is locked, `vat_date` = the booking date
(a later deduction is allowed). Accrual `income_date` = the expense `date` (or the booking date when that year is
closed); EÜR `income_date` = the payment date.

| Treatment / basis | Bill entry | Payment entry |
|---|---|---|
| `standard`, accrual | D category `cd` (`income_date`), D `meals_nondeductible` `c − cd` (`income_date`) or D `private_withdrawal` `c − cd` (no `income_date`: private shares never enter the P&L or the EÜR), D `input_vat_r` iv (`tax_key` VST, input, `vat_date`), C `payables` g (open item) | D `payables` g / C `bank` g |
| `standard`, EÜR | D `input_vat_r` iv (VST, input, `vat_date`), C `input_vat_clearing` iv | D category `cd`, D `meals_nondeductible` or `private_withdrawal` `c − cd`, D `input_vat_clearing` iv (all with `income_date` = payment date except `private_withdrawal`; the clearing line is the "input VAT paid" of the gross EÜR), C `bank` g |
| `no_input_vat` | as `standard` with `iv = 0`, `c = g` (EÜR: no bill entry) | as `standard` |
| `reverse_charge_eu_service`, accrual | per rate group of the **document** (default 19; `n_r` = the sum of the line nets at that rate): `t = q2(n_r × r / 100)`, split over the lines by largest remainder in proportion to their net for the input share and the cost (ties go to
the earlier line in document order); `ri = q2(t × vp / 100)` (0 for a small business): C `rc_vat_out_eu` t (`tax_key` RC_EU_SVC, tax, base n_r, `vat_date` = § 13b tax date), D `rc_input_vat` ri (RC_EU_SVC, input, same `vat_date`), D cost `n + (t − ri)` split by `pct` (`income_date`), C `payables` n | D `payables` n / C `bank` n |
| `reverse_charge_eu_service`, EÜR | C `rc_vat_out_eu` t, D `rc_input_vat` ri (as accrual), D `rc_vat_nondeductible` `t − ri` (no `income_date`: under EÜR the non-deductible tax becomes an expense when it is paid to the tax office with the return, section 9.6, so it must not be counted twice) | D cost `n` split by `pct` (`income_date` = payment date) / C `bank` n |
| `reverse_charge_*`, EÜR, `pct` < 100 | the bill entry above, plus the private share of the non-deductible tax `x = (t − ri) − q2((t − ri) × pct / 100)`: D `private_withdrawal` x / C `vat_paid_private` x (`income_date` = booking date; the role sits on the EÜR line "VAT paid to the tax office" and reduces it, because the return payment of 9.6 counts the whole `t − ri` as an expense) *(verify the timing)* | |
| `reverse_charge_third_country` | as the EU rows with `rc_vat_out_3c` and RC_3C_SVC | |

With `pct` below 100 a § 13b expense can differ by one cent between accrual and EÜR (accrual rounds `q2((n + nd) ×
pct)`, EÜR rounds the cost `q2(n × pct)` and the non-deductible tax share separately, because they are posted at
different times); reconciliation tests between the bases allow one cent per such expense.

§ 13b tax date *(verify)*: EU service (Abs. 1) = `service_date` (any date in the period of performance gives the same
return); third country (Abs. 2 Nr. 1) = `min(date, last day of the month after service_date)`. `service_date`
defaults to `date`. When the tax date lies in a locked month the lines keep it (allowed drift, section 11): the tax
arose in that period by law.

Changing an expense: the service posts reversals of its current bill and payment entries (`reverses_id`) and new ones
with revision `r+1`; deleting posts the reversals only. Both are refused (409 "the period is locked; book a
correction") when a **bill** entry to be reversed has its booking date in a locked month. "Changed" is decided by
comparing the would-be entry with the **stored** current entry (not with a recomputation of the old record): lines,
`document_date`, `source_number`, `counterparty_id`, `cp_vat_id`, `cp_country` (the free `text` alone never reposts); payment entries carry no
VAT line and may be reversed and reposted into the first open month (above).

### 9.5 Trips

On trip finalise: D `travel_costs` (allowance + mileage, `income_date` = trip end date) / C `private_deposit` (owner)
or `payables_employee` (a team member). No VAT. Reopen of a trip posts the reversal (same lock rule). Trip receipts
are expenses and post as 9.4.

### 9.6 VAT payments, refunds, special prepayment

* From the VAT return page ("record payment"): D `vat_prepayments` amount / C `bank` (refund: the reverse). `income_date`
  = payment date (the gross EÜR counts VAT paid to the tax office as an expense and refunds as income).
* Special prepayment (Sondervorauszahlung, [`ustva.md`](ustva.md)): payment D `vat_special_prepayment` / C `bank`
  (`income_date` = payment date). When the December (or Q4) return is finalised: D `vat_prepayments` / C
  `vat_special_prepayment` with the Kz 39 amount (moves the credit).
* The cent difference between form tax and booked tax stays on the VAT accounts until the annual return (out of scope);
  the trial balance shows it as "VAT rounding to be settled by the annual return".

### 9.7 Income-related taxes

| Case | Lines |
|---|---|
| Gewerbesteuer prepayment / final payment | D `trade_tax_expense` / C `bank` (`income_date` = payment). For EÜR this is the whole treatment. Not deductible for income tax (§ 4 Abs. 5b EStG): the EÜR report shows it on its own line and adds it back *(verify line)* |
| Gewerbesteuer provision (accrual, at year closing) | D `trade_tax_expense` (`income_date` = the closing date, so the year's P&L carries it) / C `trade_tax_provision`, amount = the estimate from `core/tax.py` **minus the trade-tax prepayments already expensed in that year** (the user confirms or edits it); next year's payment D `trade_tax_provision` / C `bank` (no `income_date`) up to the provision; a payment above the provision posts the difference D `trade_tax_expense` (`income_date` = payment date), a payment below it releases the rest D `trade_tax_provision` / C `tax_refunds` (`income_date` = payment date), so the provision account ends at 0 |
| Income tax, Soli, church tax of a sole proprietor | D `private_withdrawal` / C `bank` (never an expense) |
| Corporate tax (GmbH) | D `corporate_tax_expense` / C `bank`; provision and its settlement like the trade tax (a payment below the provision releases the rest to `tax_refunds`) |
| Tax refunds | the reverse lines; GewSt refunds to `tax_refunds` |

These are offered as **entry templates** in the manual entry dialog (prefilled roles, the user types amount and date).
The Money page's reserve keeps working from `core/tax.py`; it additionally shows prepayments already booked.

### 9.8 Manual entries

Reversals of trips, manual entries and template entries follow 7.8: booked on `max(original booking date, first open
day)`, with the original `income_date` while that year is open and the booking date once it is closed.

Free, balanced entries on any account of the year's chart (`accounts` list), boss and accountant. Rules: at least two
lines; text required; each line's account must be in the chart; a line with a `tax_key` needs `tax_kind`, `vat_date`
and (for `tax`/`base_only`) `tax_base_cents`; the booking date must be in an unlocked month; `vat_date` in a locked
month is refused for manual entries (no manual drift). Correction of a manual entry = a reversal (button "reverse")
plus a new entry. Templates: opening balance, private withdrawal/deposit, VAT payment, the taxes of 9.7, depreciation
(AfA, amount typed by the user), bank fees.

## 10. Editing rules for source records

| Record | Rule |
|---|---|
| invoice, draft | editable and deletable as today |
| invoice, issued | **frozen**: PUT changing anything but the status labels, `due_date`, `notes`, `reference`, attachments → 409; DELETE → 409; `status: cancelled` → 409 "issue a cancellation (credit note)"; legacy `status: paid` + `paid_date` → translated into one payment of the open amount on that date (contract: [`credit-notes-payments.md`](credit-notes-payments.md)); `status: paid` without a date → 422 |
| invoice status | `sent` / `accepted` / `rejected` stay user-set labels; derived values win in this order: `cancelled` (a `storno` credited everything), then `paid` (open amount ≤ 0 and some cash received), then the label; stored for old clients |
| credit note | issued on creation (no draft in v1); frozen like an invoice |
| expense, trip | section 9.4 / 9.5 (repost while open, 409 when locked) |
| import (`importer/service.py`) | imported invoices are posted like issued ones on their dates; undo of a batch posts reversals while the months are open, else 409 |

These rules apply from the release on, with or without accounting initialised (issued invoices are always frozen);
postings start only after initialisation (section 16).

## 11. Period lock

* `period_locks` rows lock whole months, in order: locking month m requires every earlier month of the journal (from
  the accounting start) to be locked. No unlock (GoBD *Festschreibung*).
* Triggers: finalising a VAT return locks its months ([`ustva.md`](ustva.md)); manual "lock up to" (boss,
  accountant); the year closing locks December.
* Effect: no entry has a booking date in a locked month, except the closing date 31 December of a fiscal year that
  is not closed (section 7.8; manual year-end entries such as depreciation, accruals and provisions use it too). A late document is **not refused**: its entry is booked on
  the first day of the first unlocked month (section 7.8), so bills, payments and `paid_on` dates of a filed month can
  still be entered. Reversing an entry whose booking date is in a locked month is refused (409); corrections are new
  entries — except the payment entry of an expense or trip, which carries no VAT line (9.4). Invoices and credit notes are issued with a date in an open month (a back-dated issue into a locked month
  is refused, 409); the importer follows the same rules.
* `vat_date` in a locked month — the **allowed drift cases** (automatic postings only; manual entries never):
  1. a Soll issue whose performance date lies in a locked month (an invoice written after the return);
  2. a cash event (payment, refund, reversal, write-off reversal) dated in a locked month that changes the due VAT
     (Ist always; Soll when cash above the current gross is refunded or a write-off is reversed);
  3. a § 13b tax date (EU service period, third-country date) in a locked month;
  4. an `eu_b2b_service` performance date in a locked month (Kz 21, ZM).
  Input VAT (`VST`) never drifts: its `vat_date` = max(its date, first day of the first unlocked month) — the booking
  date within the year, the first open month of the next year for a closing-date entry (a later deduction is
  allowed). The same applies to every other non-drift VAT line of a closing-date entry; a manual closing-date entry
  with a `vat_date` in a locked month is refused (422). Credit
  notes and write-offs are dated in an open month and never drift — except the write-off reversal record, which is
  dated the payment date (drift case 2). Drift lines are listed on the return of their
  period and call for an amended return ([`ustva.md`](ustva.md)); ZM drift likewise ([`zm.md`](zm.md)).
* `income_date` follows the document date within an open fiscal year; in a closed year it becomes the booking date.

## 12. Opening balances (accrual)

On 1 January of the accounting start (and every following year after the closing): the opening entry D/C the balance
roles against `opening_balance`. At initialisation the user types bank, cash, equity and other balances (manual
template); open receivables come from the backfill (section 16).

## 13. Year closing (accrual) and year end (EÜR)

* Accrual: "close year" needs all 12 months locked except December (closing locks it) — December may already be
  locked by the December/Q4 return; closing-date entries (section 7.8) are allowed until the year is closed. It posts the provisions the
  user confirms (9.7), transfers the balances of the VAT accounts (`vat_out_*`, `input_vat_*`, `rc_*`,
  `vat_prepayments`, `vat_special_prepayment`) to `vat_previous_year` (settled by the annual return and its payment,
  a manual template), then the closing entry (balance accounts to `closing_balance`, P&L to `profit_carryforward` /
  `equity`), sets `closed_at`, and creates the next year's `fiscal_years` row (same bases unless changed) and opening
  entry.
* EÜR: no closing entry; "end year" locks December and creates the next year's row. Open items stay open.

## 14. Reports (read only, from the journal)

1. **Journal** (filter by date, account, source, tax key; the entry with its lines and hash state).
2. **Account ledger** per account with running balance.
3. **Trial balance** (*Summen- und Saldenliste*) per month or year; shows the VAT rounding note (9.6).
4. **EÜR** (EÜR basis), gross form like the Anlage EÜR: revenue (by tax key), VAT received, VAT refunds from the tax
   office; expenses by `eur_line`, input VAT paid, VAT paid to the tax office; profit. Lines with `income_date` in the
   year. Each `eur_line` in the chart file carries `deductible: true|false`: **profit = Σ of the income lines on
   deductible lines**; non-deductible business expenses (`meals_nondeductible`, `trade_tax_expense`) are shown on
   their own lines below the profit as "not deductible, added back", and GewSt refunds (`tax_refunds`) likewise as
   non-taxable. Private shares (`private_withdrawal`) are never in the EÜR. Lines on `vat_prepayments` with an
   `income_date` are split by sign into "VAT paid to the tax office" (debit) and "VAT refunded" (credit);
   `vat_special_prepayment` payments belong to "VAT paid"; lines without `income_date` (the December credit of the
   special prepayment) are ignored. *(verify)* the line list against the
   form of the year; the app does not file the Anlage EÜR.
5. **P&L and balance sheet** (accrual): by account class of the chart; simple form (no HGB layout in v1). Under
   accrual every line's `income_date` lies in the entry's fiscal year (`JournalService` refuses other lines with 422),
   so the P&L result equals the balance-sheet result of the year; before the year closing the balance sheet shows the
   results of earlier unclosed years as a separate "result of earlier years not yet closed" line. The EÜR route
   answers 422 for an accrual year and P&L / balance sheet answer 422 for an EÜR year (EÜR may carry an `income_date`
   in another year, e.g. the 10-day rule for recurring payments).
6. **Open items**: receivables and payables per document (gross − credit notes − payments), age.
7. **VAT account reconciliation**: per period, the VAT account balances vs. the filed returns.

The Money page (`calc.finance`) switches to the journal once accounting is initialised for the year
(revenue, costs, VAT per month from `income_date` / `vat_date`), keeping its response shape; a reconciliation test
compares the old computation and the journal for documents that the old code could represent. Until then (accounting
not initialised) the old computation nets credit notes on their own date, also against an unpaid invoice under the
cash basis, and drops a storno'd invoice entirely, also when it was paid in an earlier year — known differences that
the journal-based figures do not have; the page says "approximate until accounting is set up".

## 16. Initialisation and backfill

`permita38 accounting init` (CLI) and a web wizard (boss) — one transaction, idempotent through `event_key`:

1. The user chooses chart, income basis, VAT basis (default Soll), frequency, Dauerfrist, small-business status and
   the **accounting start** (default 1 January of the current year), stored in the settings as
   `accounting.start` (a date, first day of a month; set once by the initialisation). Locks, the lock order and the
   "before the accounting start" rules read it; months before it are never locked and hold no journal.
2. Creates the `fiscal_years` row and, for accrual, the opening entry from the balances the user types.
3. Writes the `issued` snapshot for every issued invoice that lacks one (rates from the document, regime from the
   `kleinunternehmer` setting unless `vat_regular_from` says otherwise, customer data as now).
4. Invoices issued before the start and **open at the start date** (gross minus payments dated before the start):
   the **pre-start baseline** = the 9.2 targets of the invoice with its pre-start payments and credits. One opening
   entry dated the start date posts only its balance-sheet part, every line against `opening_balance`, no `vat_date` or
   `income_date` (that VAT and income belong to earlier returns): accrual → `receivables`, and under Ist
   `vat_not_due_r` for the VAT not yet due; EÜR/Soll → `vat_soll_clearing_r` for the declared VAT not yet received;
   EÜR/Ist → nothing. Their payments dated on/after the start then post normally (Ist VAT, EÜR income).
5. Invoices issued on/after the start: the issue entry; payments and credit notes stored before the initialisation
   post as their events in date order; legacy `paid` + `paid_date` → one payment of the open amount on that date.
   Expenses dated before the start but paid on/after it: accrual → opening payable; EÜR → an opening entry D
   `opening_balance` / C `input_vat_clearing` for their input VAT (already deducted in an earlier return), then the
   payment event posts the cost and clears it.
5a. Any line whose `vat_date` lies before the accounting start (a Soll invoice, a § 13b bill or an `eu_b2b_service`
   invoice in January for a December service) belongs to a return or ZM outside the app. In the backfill such lines go
   to the **review list** ("declared outside the app"); after initialisation the same case posts normally, keeps its
   `vat_date`, falls into no app return, and is listed with a warning in the report "before the accounting start".
   Expenses dated on/after the start with `paid_on` before the start (prepaid) also go to the review list.
   Invoices issued before the start but fully settled before it get their pre-start baseline (step 4) lazily, when a
   later credit note or refund arrives.
5b. **Returns already filed outside the app** for months on/after the start: the wizard asks per period for the filed
   Kz 83 (and Kz 39) and the filing date and stores them as `vat_returns` with `status = final`, `filed_on`, `external =
   true` and the entered figures; the months are locked after the backfill. Differences between the entered figures
   and the journal show as drift on those returns, and the special-prepayment base uses the entered Kz 83.
6. Expenses dated on/after the start: tax treatment, in order: an explicit `tax_treatment`; else `reverse_charge = true`
   with the supplier's `vat_mode` (`eu_reverse_charge` → `reverse_charge_eu_service`, `third_country_reverse_charge` →
   `reverse_charge_third_country`); `reverse_charge = true` without a supplier → **review list**; supplier
   `small_business` → `no_input_vat`; else `standard`. `reverse_charge = true` with a stored VAT ≠ 0 → **review list**
   (the stored figures were typed, not self-assessed).
7. Trips finalised on/after the start post.
8. Legacy `cancelled` invoices: unpaid → issue entry plus a storno credit note dated the cancel date (unknown: the
   invoice date) with number from the credit-note sequence and the text "migrated cancellation"; paid-then-cancelled →
   **review list** (they changed past figures; CHANGELOG note).
9. The review list blocks finishing the wizard until each row is resolved (choose a treatment / confirm / create the
   credit note). Nothing is guessed.
10. Mapping for writes after init: `reverse_charge` sent by an old client sets `tax_treatment` from the supplier's
    `vat_mode` (default EU); `tax_treatment` wins when both are sent.

Before initialisation the VAT and accounting pages show "set up accounting" and nothing posts; issued invoices are
still frozen and credit notes and payments are recorded (they post on initialisation).

## 17. Rights

New areas in `core/rights.py`: `accounting` (journal, reports, manual entries, locks, fiscal years, init, DATEV) and
`vat` (returns, ZM). Matrix: boss (A, A); **accountant (A, A)** (maintainer decision: full bookkeeping); employee
(N, N). Payments and credit notes require write on `invoices` **or** `accounting` (the accountant has the latter); reading them needs read on `invoices` (they are part of the invoice).
`init` and `close year` need boss. The rbac route table, `docs/roles.md` and the every-route-as-every-role test are
extended. API tokens: read scopes follow the existing token rules; no write to `accounting`/`vat` through tokens in v1.

## 20. Test plan

* **Oracle:** A1–A18 from the fixture through the real services; expected entries compared line by line.
* **Independent oracle:** a hypothesis test generates invoices (rates 0/7/19/16, cents), credit notes, partial
  payments and refunds in random order, under all four bases, and checks with `fractions.Fraction` and its own
  rounding helpers (not `core/money.py`): every entry balances; every cash event moves exactly its amount through
  the 9.2 targets (ΔT + Δ(P − T) = p), also after credit notes, partial refunds and write-off reversals; after full settlement the
  recognised VAT and base equal the current document; under Soll the due VAT of an invoice in total is never below the
  VAT of the cash it keeps (Σ_r D_r ≥ Σ_r TV_r; per rate group it can be, see A18); Σ VAT lines by `vat_date` equals the independently computed due VAT; order of payments does not
  change the totals.
* **Guarantees:** UPDATE/DELETE refused by the database in every dialect (contract test); `event_key` idempotence;
  gapless `entry_no` under concurrent posting; hash chain detects a changed row and a forked sequence.
* **Locks:** no booking date in a locked month for any event type; late documents move to the first open month
  (A15) and drift only in the four allowed cases (A16); reversals of locked entries refused; lock order; no unlock
  route.
* **Editing rules:** every 409/422 of section 10 incl. the legacy `paid` translation and `cancelled` refusal.
* **Backfill:** each rule of section 16 incl. the review list; re-running init is a no-op.
* **Reports:** trial balance sums to 0; EÜR profit equals Σ income lines on deductible EÜR lines; open items equal gross − credits − payments.
* **Reconciliation:** `finance()` old vs. journal for `standard` documents.
* **Rights:** every new route as every role (rbac table).
* **Randomized suites:** the stateful machine models frozen invoices, payments, credit notes and locks; the web
  e2e_random models follow (every API model is updated, backend and web).
